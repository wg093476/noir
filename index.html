How to take your photos (read first)
Before you start
  • Use your real, physical ID — not a photocopy, a screenshot, or a photo of a screen.
  • It must be valid (not expired), and the name you type below must match the ID exactly.
  • A driver's license, state ID or passport works. For a passport, photograph the page with your photo.
Photo 1 — the front of your ID
  1. Lay the ID flat on a plain, dark background — a black table, black shirt or dark paper. No patterns, no other cards or objects.
  2. Use bright, even light. Daylight near a window is best. Turn flash off — it causes glare on the plastic.
  3. Hold your phone straight above the ID, parallel to it (not at an angle).
  4. Fill most of the photo with the ID, but keep all four corners visible with a little background around the edges.
  5. Tap the screen on the ID to focus, hold still, and take the photo.
  6. Zoom in and check: every word, number, date and your face on the ID must be sharp and readable. If there's a white shine on any text, tilt the ID slightly and retake.
  7. Don't cover any part with your fingers, and don't crop, edit or use filters.
Photo 2 — a selfie holding your ID
  1. Hold the ID next to your face at chin level, with the front facing the camera. Don't cover your face with it.
  2. Look straight at the camera with your whole face in the picture and eyes open.
  3. Take off sunglasses, hats and masks. Regular glasses are fine if there's no glare on them.
  4. Face a window or lamp so the light is on your face. Don't stand with a bright window behind you.
  5. A plain wall behind you is best.
  6. Use your normal camera — no filters, beauty mode or edits. Make sure your face and the ID are both in focus.
Most common reasons a check fails
  • Blurry photo, glare over the text, or a corner cut off
  • Name typed differently from the ID (use your full legal name)
  • Expired ID, or a photo of a screen / copy instead of the real card
  • Face hidden, too dark, or sunglasses/hat in the selfie
