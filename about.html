Digital gift cards. Delivered instantly.

FAQ

How fast do I get my code?

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay orders usually show the code on screen within seconds, and email you a copy. Crypto orders are emailed once the network confirms the payment. That's a few minutes for ETH, USDT and USDC, and 10–60 minutes for Bitcoin.

Why is there a service fee?

The fee covers payment processing and running the store. It's always shown separately before you pay, and the total you see is the total you pay.

How do I pay?

Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link are right on the card page. You can also pay by credit or debit card through Stripe's secure checkout, or with Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC and more through NOWPayments.

Is it safe?

Payments are handled by Stripe and NOWPayments. We never see your full card number. Codes come straight from our supplier and are shown only to you and sent only to your email.

Which countries do the cards work in?

Our cards are US cards and work on US accounts and stores.

I lost my code.

Check your email, including spam, for the delivery message. Still can't find it? Email us with the address you used and the time of your order.

My code doesn't work.

Email us within 7 days with a screenshot of the error. We'll check it with our supplier and replace or refund it if it's faulty. See the refund policy.

Can I get a refund?

A delivered code can't be returned because it's usable right away. If we can't deliver your code, you're refunded automatically. Full details are in the refund policy.

Is there a limit per order?

Yes. To keep everyone safe from fraud, each order is one card up to $100.
