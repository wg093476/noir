Privacy policy

Last updated

NOIR collects as little as possible: only what's needed to deliver your gift card and keep orders safe.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to deliver your code and help with your order.
  • Order details: the brand, amount, price, time and payment status.
  • Payment details are handled by Stripe or NOWPayments. We never see or store your full card number, and we never hold your crypto wallet keys.
  • Account and limit data: if you sign in, your email and account level. To enforce daily spending limits we keep a running total of each day's purchases linked to your account, or for guests to a random browser ID (a cookie), a scrambled version of your IP address, your email and a fingerprint of your card from Stripe. These totals are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Cookies: one to keep you signed in and one random browser ID for spending limits. No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Basic usage data: anonymous page-view counts through Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies. We also keep standard server logs, such as error logs and searches that found no card, to keep the site running.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that make an order work:

  • Stripe: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link payments, and fraud screening
  • NOWPayments: crypto payments
  • Reloadly: our gift card supplier, which receives your email to deliver the code
  • Vercel: hosting
  • Upstash: our database for accounts and daily limits
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes by email

We don't sell your information or use it for ads.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for customer support, fraud prevention, and tax and accounting rules.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing . We'll keep only what the law requires.

Children

NOIR isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
